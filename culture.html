Our Ethos

Excellence is a habit, not an act.

At Ab Initio Legal LLP, the way we work with each other shapes the way we work for our clients. The same three principles that define our client service — Trust, Timely and Prompt Execution, and Transparency — define our workplace.

We are a close-knit team. Partners lead from the front in court, doors stay open, and every associate and intern is encouraged to take ownership, ask questions and argue their point — on paper and before the bench.

“From the beginning” is not just our name — it is how we approach every matter, and every member of our team.
