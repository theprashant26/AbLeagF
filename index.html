Ab Initio · “From the Beginning”

A full-service firm, built on litigation.

Established in 2016, Ab Initio Legal LLP is a full-service law firm headquartered in New Delhi, with a pan-India presence through associate offices across the country. Our practice is driven by Partners with varied experience across judicial and quasi-judicial fora in different jurisdictions — offering clients a cost-effective, result-oriented and coordinated response to the unpredictability of litigation.

“By combining strategic foresight, meticulous preparation and practical advice, we ensure our clients are equipped to face the uncertainties of litigation.”
