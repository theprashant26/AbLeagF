About the Firm

Established in 2016, a full-service law firm headquartered in New Delhi, with a pan-India presence.

Overview

Litigation-driven. Client-focused.

Established in 2016, Ab Initio Legal LLP is a full-service law firm headquartered in New Delhi, with a pan-India presence through associate offices across the country. The firm has experience across Dispute Resolution, Commercial & Corporate Litigation, International and Domestic Arbitration, Insolvency, High Court of Delhi (Original and Appellate Sides), Blue & White-Collar Crimes, Civil & Criminal Trials, Writs and Appeals, Consumer matters and SLPs before the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India.

Our practice is driven by Partners with varied experience across judicial and quasi-judicial fora in different jurisdictions. We aim to provide a cost-effective, result-oriented and coordinated approach to the unpredictability attached to litigation and dispute resolution.

Our lawyers bring a cumulative experience of more than five decades, with seamless cross-practice experience. Our legal strategies are designed to keep our clients’ business operations uninterrupted by the dispute resolution process, while protecting their long-term interests.

Year in Review · 2024-25

A year of consistent results.

410+Court appearances

During 2024-25, Ab Initio Legal LLP maintained a strong, consistent and results-driven litigation practice across multiple judicial and quasi-judicial forums in India, representing financial institutions, private corporations, multinational companies, start-ups and individuals.

Commercial litigation & arbitration - interim and final reliefs in contractual disputes, shareholder matters, recovery actions and complex commercial claims, including favourable arbitral awards, successful defence of jurisdictional challenges and protection of high-value commercial and aviation assets.

Insolvency & Bankruptcy - critical reliefs before the NCLT including initiation of proceedings under Section 7, asset protection during moratorium, and favourable orders relating to exchange deeds and shareholder rights.

Criminal (White-Collar and Blue-Collar) - advising and representing clients during investigations, preliminary enquiries under the BNSS and trial-stage proceedings, securing procedural safeguards and interim protection.

Constitutional & public law - significant questions of law before the High Courts and the Supreme Court, with a strong focus on trial-court litigation, cross-examinations, injunctions and urgent interim reliefs.
